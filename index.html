Demoing your extension

Compare both protection modes

This page's job is always the same: attempt a real clipboard write. What happens to that write depends entirely on how the extension is configured — switch modes in its options and re-run the "Copy fix command" step above to show the difference live.

Clipboard API Only

Blocks every programmatic clipboard write, unconditionally — content is never inspected.

  • Expected result here: the write attempt above fails or is silently dropped every time, regardless of scenario.
  • Blind spot to call out: a lure that asks the target to manually select and copy text (no script involved) still gets through in this mode.

AI Classifier

Lets the write land, immediately quarantines the clipboard behind a placeholder, screens the text with local heuristics and a cloud classifier, then restores it if benign.

  • Expected result here: click "Check clipboard now" right after copying and again a moment later — you should see the placeholder, then a final verdict, since every payload on this page is written to mirror a real ClickFix command.
  • What it catches that Mode 1 misses: nothing extra for scripted copies — this mode is about deciding intent, not just blocking the mechanism.
Field guide

Spot it. Stop it.

Worth keeping regardless of which scenario or protection mode you're demoing.

Red flags in the lure

  • It asks you to open Run, PowerShell, or Terminal. No legitimate verification, video call, or browser update ever needs that.
  • It gives you keyboard steps, not a button. "Press Win+R, then Ctrl+V, then Enter" is the whole attack, spelled out as instructions.
  • The fix is oddly specific and urgent. Framing creates just enough pressure to skip the "wait, why?" moment.
  • You can't verify what's actually on your clipboard until you've already pasted it somewhere.

What to do instead

  • Never paste into Run, PowerShell, or a terminal because a webpage told you to. That single habit defeats this entire attack class.
  • Close the tab. A real verification or update failure survives you leaving and coming back.
  • Go to the real service directly by typing the known URL yourself.
  • If you already pasted and ran something, disconnect from the network and report it immediately.
